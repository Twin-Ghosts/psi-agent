3.3.1 In accordance with the provisions of laws and regulations, you are required to complete real-name authentication when using certain functions of the Software and related services. Real-name authentication is an important basis for determining account ownership. We will collect your true identity information (such as your name and ID card number) in accordance with the requirements of laws and regulations for completing real-name authentication.

3.3.2 The aforementioned information includes sensitive personal information, and you may refuse to provide it. If you refuse to provide it, you may not be able to use the functions requiring real-name authentication, but this will not affect your use of other functions and services.

3.3.3 The information involved in registering and managing accounts is closely related to user management and use of the services (such as verifying the user’s identity). Please be sure to submit lawful, true, and valid information. When the aforementioned information changes, please update it in a timely manner.

3.6 In order to provide continuous services and ensure service quality, during the period of users’ use of the services, we will record and store the relevant information of users’ login and use of the services, for example:

3.6.1 Operation records. We will receive and record users’ operation records and related information when using the services, such as identity ID, operation time, operation content, and IP address.

3.6.2 Log data. Including users’ browser information and type, device information (for example, Linux: /var/lib/dbus/machine-id, macOS: IOPlatformUUID, Windows: registry MachineGuid/wmic UUID), browser fingerprint information (used for personalized recommendations), the date and time of user input and model output, etc.

3.6.3 Intelligent conversation. The intelligent conversation function is designed to understand users’ input content and form output that can be executed as tasks. The input content proactively sent by users or authorized by users for us to obtain through the dialogue box will be collected by us and sent to third-party Large Models, which will process it independently and directly return the output content to users. In order to provide users with a coherent and intelligent service experience, realize subsequent operations, and ensure service quality, we will collect from users’ local devices the conversation records between users and the services, including users’ input content, output content, and the conversation topics formed based on the aforementioned information. When users proactively paste content from the local device’s system clipboard into the intelligent dialogue box, we will collect users’ clipboard information locally.

3.6.4 Intelligent operation. The intelligent operation function is designed to assist users in executing corresponding tasks or completing corresponding operations based on the output content formed by “intelligent conversation”. Depending on the type of operation, the data processing methods are as follows:

(1) Local operations: if the task corresponding to the output content is an operation that can be completed within the user’s local device environment, such as opening local files or modifying system settings, the corresponding output content will be executed by tools on the user’s local device. During this operation, we only provide technical assistance such as tool invocation and will not collect users’ information.

(2) Third-party software operations: if the task corresponding to the output content involves operations on third parties, such as visiting websites or operating software, the corresponding output content will be executed by third-party software on the user’s device (for example, a browser). During this process, the interaction information between the user and the third party is directly collected and independently processed by the third party, and we will not collect information, unless the user proactively submits new input content to us through the service dialogue box during the process.

3.6.5 Remote functions. To facilitate users’ remote use of the intelligent operation function, we provide remote functions. Users can conduct remote conversations through channels such as mini programs and apps:

(1) Mini program remote function: it is designed to provide users with the ability to remotely send input content to Haitun through the mini program. The input content sent by users through the Haitun mini program or authorized by users for us to obtain will be processed in accordance with Article 3.6.3 (Intelligent Conversation) of this Policy.

(2) App remote function: it is designed to provide users with the ability to remotely send input content to Haitun through the app. The input content sent by users through the app or authorized by users for us to obtain will be processed in accordance with Article 3.6.3 (Intelligent Conversation) of this Policy.

3.7 Memory Function

3.7.1 In order to make the output content of intelligent conversation better fit users’ needs and improve the coherence, personalization, and service experience of intelligent conversation and intelligent operation, we provide users with cross-session memory capability in the memory function.

3.7.2 The memory function will, based on the conversation content between users and Haitun, automatically extract and remember personal information of reference value therein through Large Models and related artificial intelligence technologies (for example, identity, occupation, projects, preferences, etc. proactively provided by users, or information that users can ask Haitun to remember in conversations, such as users’ work background, personal background, current concerns, and recent developments, collectively referred to as “Memory Data”), and update it periodically. Users may also import memory data from other AI systems in accordance with the relevant guidelines of the services, so as to synchronize their usage habits on other AI systems.

3.7.3 During subsequent conversations or task execution, the services may use the Memory Data related to the current request as background information and use it together with users’ input content to generate output content or execute tasks.

3.7.4 Users may, in the “Settings - Memory - View and Edit Memory” page of the service client, issue instructions through conversations to add, delete, or correct Haitun’s Memory Data about users.

3.7.5 If users need to turn off the memory function, they may do so in the “Settings - Memory” page of the service client. After turning it off, the services will no longer extract Memory Data.
